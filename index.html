🚨 PPH EMERGENCY — ACT NOW

Use the approved clinical protocol and seek appropriate help immediately. This guide does not replace clinical care.

1

RECOGNIZE

Identify abnormal postpartum bleeding and assess the woman's condition and vital signs according to the approved protocol.

2

CALL FOR HELP

Alert the appropriate maternity/emergency team immediately.

3

START THE APPROVED PPH RESPONSE

Follow the facility's current PPH protocol and initiate recommended emergency interventions without unnecessary delay.

M Massage the uterus
O Oxytocic
T Tranexamic acid
I IV fluids
V Vaginal/genital tract examination
E Escalate care
4

MONITOR

Continuously monitor bleeding, vital signs, general condition and response to treatment.

5

ESCALATE / REFER

If bleeding persists, the woman becomes unstable, or higher-level care is required, activate the referral pathway immediately.
